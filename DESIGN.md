# DESIGN.md — Redstone x Saltgrass proposal page

Identity: REDSTONE ENERGY's aesthetic (per Dan, Aug 14 2026), carried over from Kamal's 7-Layer Plan PDF. Energy Plus is the smaller partner in the lockup. Redstone logo extracted from the PDF (scratchpad pdfimg_p0_0.jpg) and embedded as a data URI; Energy Plus white logo appears once, in the red CTA band.

## Scene
An executive opens this from a text or email, on a phone or in a conference room. It must read as a continuation of the printed 7-Layer Plan Terry already holds: same red, same square chips, same boxed callouts, same all-caps header voice.

## Palette (Redstone light)
- Page: white; alternating sections #f6f6f7; 6px red topline at page head.
- Red #be1e2d (sampled from logo), deep #9e1826. Slate gray #54565b (logo gray) for kickers.
- Ink #1f2226, body #33383e, muted #666d75, hairlines #e3e4e6.
- Red usage: topline, act rules, square number chips, outlined callout boxes (the "Redstone outcome" box language), stat numbers, kicker lines, and the solid red CTA band with the "One call. One owner. One scoreboard." tagline.

## Type
- Display: Oswald 600 all caps (matches the condensed wordmark letterforms and the PDF's header voice). Clamp max ~4.6rem.
- Body: Barlow 400/500, 19.5px, 1.62.
- Labels: Barlow Condensed caps, red rule + slate label at the three act breaks only.

## Imagery
Custom SVG only (one wasted kilowatt splitting into three costs, red paths and square nodes). No stock restaurant photos: fake restaurants read as dishonest to a reader who owns real ones.

## Motion
Scroll reveals (translateY + fade, ease out), visible by default without JS, full reduced motion fallback.

## Bans honored
No side stripe borders, no gradient text, no glassmorphism, no identical card grids, no eyebrow on every section (three act labels total), no hero metric template.
