## What this is
A single-file landing page at slashyourenergycost.com/redstone-saltgrass/ that carries the Energy Plus proposal to Landry's / Saltgrass Steak House. It is the digital "mega report" behind Layer 6 of Redstone Energy's 7-Layer Plan. Branding: "Redstone Energy in partnership with Energy Plus."

## Audience
Terry Turney (COO level, 30+ years at Landry's, warm to Kamal) and whoever he forwards it to (CFO / procurement, cold, skeptical, will fact-check). Read on phone or laptop, likely opened from a text or email. Older executive audience: large type, high contrast, zero gimmicks.

## The one-liner
Saltgrass restaurants pay for every wasted kilowatt three times: on the meter, on the demand charge, and in the life of the equipment. Energy Plus removes the waste, proven with their own meter data, or nothing is owed.

## Narrative arc (locked with Dan, Aug 2026)
1. Insight: your best nights are your most expensive nights (crowd drives humidity and load; aging units lost the capacity to fight back; site 1 runs 20 year old equipment).
2. Villain: every wasted kilowatt bills you three times (meter, demand charges rising, equipment mortality). Escalating order; replacement cost last.
3. Prescription: one lever cuts all three. Right tool per site, white labeled (no product names ever).
4. Proof: person (Cliff, exact sentence pending sign-off), protocol (IPMVP metered verification), precedent (technology results: Simon Property Group 21.1 percent, DHL 15 percent, Anheuser-Busch 19.2 percent, labeled as technology results).
5. Ask: three step pilot, floor guarantee last line (no proof, no install, nothing owed).
